    About this tool

    Shuffles names randomly and distributes them as evenly as possible across the number of teams you specify. Click Reassign to get a different split with the same names.

    FAQ

    Are the teams always balanced?
    Yes in Auto-balance mode. With 7 people split into 2 teams you get 4 and 3.
    Can I set a fixed team size instead of a fixed number of teams?
    Open Advanced options and set Team size to Fixed size per team, then enter the number of people per team.
    Can I give teams custom names?
    Open Advanced options and enter one team name per line in the Custom team names field.
    Can I mark a team captain?
    Open Advanced options and check Mark first as captain. The first person on each team gets a ★ prefix.

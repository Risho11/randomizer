      FAQ

      Can I generate random business hours only?
      Open Advanced options and check Business hours only. This restricts the range to 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
      Can I round the time to 15-minute intervals?
      Open Advanced options and set Round to nearest to 15 minutes.
      How do I get a morning or evening time only?
      Use the Period selector — choose Morning (6am–12pm), Afternoon (12pm–6pm), Evening (6pm–10pm), or Night (10pm–6am).

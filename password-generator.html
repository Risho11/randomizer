      FAQ

      How long should my password be?
      At least 16 characters for everyday accounts, 20+ for banking or email. Longer is always stronger.
      What is Pronounceable mode?
      Generates passwords using alternating consonants and vowels, making them easier to say aloud while still being random.
      What is Passphrase mode?
      Generates a string of short random words separated by dashes and a number — e.g. oak-ran-fog-box-3921. Easier to remember than character passwords.
      Is this password generator safe to use?
      All generation happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. We never see or store your passwords.
      What does "Exclude ambiguous characters" do?
      Removes characters that look alike — 0 and O, 1 and l — making it easier to copy passwords by hand.

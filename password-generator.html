      About this tool

      FAQ

      Is this secure?
      Uses Math.random(), not cryptographically secure. For high-stakes passwords use your browser's password manager. For everyday use, a long password from this tool is far better than a weak one.
      What does "Exclude ambiguous" do?
      Removes characters that look similar — 0 and O, 1 and l — to make it easier to type manually.

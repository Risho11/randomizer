      About this tool

      Three generation modes. Standard mode produces random character passwords with configurable length, uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Pronounceable mode generates passwords using alternating consonants and vowels — still random but easier to say or type from memory. Passphrase mode produces a string of short random words separated by dashes and a number (e.g. oak-ran-fog-3921) — memorable and strong. Everything runs in your browser. No password is ever transmitted to any server.

      Common uses

      • Generating a secure password for a new account
      • Creating a passphrase that is strong but memorable
      • Producing passwords you can say aloud without spelling them out
      • Bulk password generation for testing or seeding databases

      FAQ

      How long should my password be?
      At least 16 characters for everyday accounts, 20+ for banking or email. Longer is always stronger.
      What is Pronounceable mode?
      Generates passwords using alternating consonants and vowels, making them easier to say aloud while still being random.
      What is Passphrase mode?
      Generates a string of short random words separated by dashes and a number — e.g. oak-ran-fog-box-3921. Easier to remember than character passwords.
      Is this password generator safe to use?
      All generation happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. We never see or store your passwords.
      What does "Exclude ambiguous characters" do?
      Removes characters that look alike — 0 and O, 1 and l — making it easier to copy passwords by hand.

      Which mode should I use?

      • Random characters — maximum entropy per character. Best for password managers where you never type it manually.
      • Pronounceable — nearly as strong, but you can read it to someone on a phone or type it on a device without copy-paste.
      • Passphrase — easiest to remember, excellent for master passwords or accounts you type regularly. Four short words + a number is more than enough entropy for most purposes.

      Protect more than your passwords

      A strong password is only part of staying secure online. A VPN encrypts your connection so your passwords and data can't be intercepted on public Wi-Fi. NordVPN is one of the most widely used — fast, no-logs, works on all devices.

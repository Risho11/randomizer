      About this tool

      Picks a random date between your chosen start and end dates. Every day in the range has an equal chance of being selected.

      Common uses

      • Test data with realistic dates for databases
      • Random historical date for trivia
      • Game events and randomized calendars
      • Birthday guessing games

      FAQ

      What formats are available?
      Long (January 5, 2024), short (Jan 5), ISO (2024-01-05), US, and European. Set in Advanced options.
      Can I generate multiple dates?
      Yes — up to 50 at once via Advanced options.

      About this tool

      Picks from [Yes, No] with equal probability — 50% each. With Maybe enabled, each answer has a one-in-three chance. No bias, no tricks.

      FAQ

      Is the result truly 50/50?
      Yes, by default. Select Lean Yes or Lean No from the Bias dropdown to change the probability.
      Can I get Yes/No in another language?
      Yes — choose from English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, or Portuguese using the Language selector.
      What does Emphatic response style mean?
      Instead of plain "Yes" or "No", emphatic mode gives answers like "Absolutely!" or "No way!".
      Can I replace Yes and No with custom words?
      Open Advanced options and fill in Custom "Yes" label and Custom "No" label with any words you want.
